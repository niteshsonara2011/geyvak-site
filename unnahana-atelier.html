Development path

From memory to a wearable piece

Each stage narrows the distance between an attractive visual and something that can be made, fitted, priced and worn responsibly.

01

Starting point

A memory, place, textile language or practical customer need becomes a clear design question.

02

Surface and placement

Motif scale, borders, colour balance and empty space are tested against the silhouette.

03

Fit and construction

A sample must answer how the piece moves, fastens, layers and feels across realistic use.

04

Verification

Material, maker, method, cost and timing are documented before public promises or payment.

The making conversation

Good design asks better questions.

A beautiful image is only the opening sentence. Development begins when we ask what is possible, who is making it, what the material truly is and whether the finished piece serves the person wearing it.

  • Is the cultural reference specific and respectfully understood?
  • Can the pattern be placed without losing clarity or proportion?
  • Has the sample been reviewed for movement, comfort and finishing?
  • Can every public claim be supported with evidence?
