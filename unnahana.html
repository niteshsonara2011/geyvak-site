How creation works

A conversation before a product.

Important decisions are confirmed before production begins, with no unsupported promises.

1

Share your idea

Tell us what you imagine, what you need and how you want to feel.

2

Shape the direction

References, mood, movement, fit, fabric and practical requirements are considered together.

3

Confirm the brief

Design, material, measurements, maker instructions, timeline and cost are confirmed before production.

4

Create with care

The piece develops with agreed review points and no unsupported promises.

Responsibility

Care is part of the design.

Care for the body. Dignity for the maker. Respect for the material. Clarity about what is still being developed.

Where possible, UNNAHANA explores made-to-order work, repair, restyling, repeat wear and considered production. Specific material, maker and production information is published only after verification.

A beautiful direction is only the beginning. The real work is making it honest, wearable and possible.
