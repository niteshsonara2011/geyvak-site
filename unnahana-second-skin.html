The idea

Leather lives differently on every body.

Second Skin explores structure, movement and identity through leather-led silhouettes: harness lines, corsetry, sculpted separates and objects that sit close to the body.

The direction is intentionally open. It is not organised around one prescribed way to dress, one gender, or one kind of occasion.

Development preview. These visuals establish the design world. Final materials, construction, fit, pricing, availability and production details will be confirmed before any product is offered for sale.
