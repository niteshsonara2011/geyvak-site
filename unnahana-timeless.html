The visual language

Culture carried quietly.

The collection does not need to announce every reference. Heritage can live in the placement of a border, the scale of a motif, the warmth of a colour and the ease of a silhouette.

  • Comfortable shapes that can move through a full day
  • Print placement considered with proportion and restraint
  • Colours that sit easily beside an existing wardrobe
  • Pieces intended to be styled across work and occasion
Before pre-order: final fabric, fit, lining, finishing, size range, production timing and price will be confirmed personally. The images establish the creative direction and are not finished-product photography.
